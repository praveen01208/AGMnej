# Manual SEO & Off-Page Guide for AGMCET

To achieve the best possible SEO results and secure a Knowledge Panel (like the one you showed in the screenshot), there are several steps you need to complete manually outside of the website code. 

Please follow this step-by-step guide after we finish the website optimizations.

## 1. Google Business Profile (For the Knowledge Panel)
This is the most critical step to get the large information card on the right side of Google Search.

1. Go to [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/).
2. Click **Manage now** and sign in with an official college Google account.
3. Search for "AGM College of Engineering and Technology" to see if a profile already exists.
    * If it exists, click **Request Access** to claim it.
    * If it doesn't, click **Add your business to Google**.
4. Fill out all the details accurately:
    * **Category**: Educational Institution / Engineering College
    * **Address**: Enter the exact, complete address in Chikodi.
    * **Contact Info**: Use the official phone number and website URL.
5. **Verify your profile**: Google will usually send a postcard with a code to the college address, or occasionally offer phone/video verification.
6. **Optimize**: Once verified, upload high-quality photos of the campus, add a description, and ensure hours of operation are correct.

## 2. Google Search Console & Analytics
These tools help you track traffic and tell Google directly about your website.

1. **Google Search Console**:
    * Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about).
    * Add your domain (e.g., `agmcet.edu.in`).
    * Verify ownership (usually via a DNS TXT record or by uploading an HTML file to the server).
    * Go to the **Sitemaps** tab and submit `https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml`.
2. **Google Analytics**:
    * Go to [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com/).
    * Create a property for the website.
    * You'll get a Measurement ID (e.g., `G-XXXXXXX`). We will need to add this ID to the website code later.

## 3. Bing Places for Business
Don't ignore Bing users! Bing also has its own knowledge panel.

1. Go to [Bing Places](https://www.bingplaces.com/).
2. You can actually use the "Import from Google My Business" feature to instantly copy your verified Google Business Profile over to Bing. This saves a lot of time.

## 4. Social Media Profiles (Entity Building)
Search engines use social media profiles to confirm that the college is a real, prominent entity.

1. Create official accounts on:
    * **LinkedIn** (Create a "Company Page" for the college)
    * **Facebook**
    * **Instagram**
    * **YouTube**
2. Ensure the "About" section on all these profiles links directly back to the official website.
3. *Note: I have added placeholders for these links in the website code. Once you create them, we can update the links on the site.*

## 5. Local Education Directories
Getting listed on other websites helps build "Backlinks", which increases the site's authority.

* Submit the college's details (Name, Address, Phone, Website) to educational portals like:
    * Shiksha.com
    * Collegedunia
    * Careers360
    * JustDial (for local Karnataka/Belagavi reach)
* **Crucial Rule**: Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP) are exactly the same across all these sites.
